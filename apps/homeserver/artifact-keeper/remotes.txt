# The cache's remote repositories: one upstream per line. The one list both
# cache-remotes.sh (which creates them on cache-1) and the client playbook
# (which points machines at them, plan step 4) read. Adding an upstream:
# agents/skills/add-a-cache-remote/.
#
# Columns: key, format, cap in GB (10^9 bytes), upstream URL.
# Each cap evicts its own repository's least recently used entries. The caps are
# generous and add up to more than the disk on purpose (owner, 2026-11-17): the
# hard limit is the instance-wide ceiling, STORAGE_QUOTA_BYTES in compose.yaml
# (80 GB on a 100 GB disk), and a 30-day idle rule, made by cache-remotes.sh,
# drains entries nobody fetched in that time. No cap may exceed the ceiling.
#
