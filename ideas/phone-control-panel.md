# Phone control panel

**Status:** idea
**In one line:** an old Android phone running postmarketOS (a Linux distribution for phones) as a small dashboard at home, with an easy way to shut the server down.
**Why try it:** people at home could shut the server down without needing to know Xen Orchestra, and it is a spare phone to play with.
**Needs first:** nothing yet; the details are still to explore.
